Sentinel // stop-loss & take-profit, executed by Reactive

Arm an order once and forget it. A reactive contract on Reactive Lasna watches the price feed on Ethereum Sepolia; when your price is hit it fires a callback to the vault, which releases your escrowed funds. No bot, no keeper, no off-chain watcher.
